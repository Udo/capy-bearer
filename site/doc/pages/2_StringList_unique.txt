:sig
DValue::unique() (legacy `StringList` page)

:content
Removed as a separate StringList API. List-shaped `DValue::unique()` returns first-occurrence strings, preserving order.

:see
0_DValue

:example capy render
var items := split("one,two,one", ",")
print(join(unique(items), ","), "\n")
:example cpp render
StringList items = split("one,two,one", ",");
print(join(items.unique(), ","), "\n");
