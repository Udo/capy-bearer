:title
job_result

:sig
DValue job_result(u64 job_id)

:params
job : the Capy `dval` job ID. C++ `.uce` uses a `u64` ID.

:returns
a `dval` status map. A completed job includes `result`.

:content
`job_result()` checks a job with a short bounded wait.

Use it to poll after `shell_spawn()` or `http_request_async()`. In Capy, pass the job ID as `dval`. In C++ `.uce`, pass the `u64` job ID shown above.

:see
>sys

:example capy render
var spec := dval({"cmd": "printf result-data", "timeout_ms": 1000})
var job := shell_spawn(spec)
job_await(job, 3000)
print(dval_get_by_path(job_result(job), "result/stdout"), "\n")

:example cpp render
DValue spec; spec["cmd"] = "printf result-data"; spec["timeout_ms"] = (f64)1000;
u64 job = shell_spawn(spec);
job_await(job, 3000);
print(job_result(job)["result"]["stdout"].to_string(), "\n");
