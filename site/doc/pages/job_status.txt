
:sig
DValue job_status(u64 job_id)

:params
job : the Capy `dval` job ID. Capy uses a `u64` ID.

:returns
a `dval` status map

:content
`job_status()` checks a job without waiting. The status map can include `state`, `done`, `kind`, `pid`, and `job_id`.

`state` can be `pending`, `running`, `done`, `failed`, `cancelled`, or `missing`. Use it to poll without waiting for a result.

In Capy, pass the job ID as `dval`. In Capy, pass the `u64` job ID shown above.

:see
>sys
shell_spawn
job_result
shell_exec
job_await
shell_escape
job_cancel
:example capy render
var spec := dval({"cmd": "printf x", "timeout_ms": 1000})
var job := shell_spawn(spec)
job_await(job, 3000)
print("state: ", job_status(job)["state"], "\n")
