:sig
DValue::sort() (legacy `StringList` page)

:content
Removed as a separate StringList API. List-shaped `DValue::sort()` returns a new ascending string-sorted list.

:see
0_DValue

:example capy render
var items := split("two,one", ",")
print(join(sort(items), ","), "\n")
:example cpp render
StringList items = split("two,one", ",");
print(join(items.sort(), ","), "\n");
