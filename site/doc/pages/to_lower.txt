
:sig
String to_lower(String s)

:params
s : C++ text to convert

:returns
`s` with uppercase ASCII characters changed to lowercase

:see
>string
lower
regex_split
to_upper
regex_replace
trim
regex_search_all
:capy-status cpp-specific

:content
`to_lower()` is a C++ API. It normalizes ASCII text. It does not convert Unicode case.

Capy does not expose `to_lower`. Use `lower(value)`.

:example cpp render
print(to_lower("BEARER"), "\n");
