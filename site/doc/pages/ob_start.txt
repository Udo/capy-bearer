:sig
function ob_start()

:content
`ob_start()` starts a new output buffer.

Later `print()` calls write to this buffer. Use `ob_get_close()` to get its text, or `ob_close()` to discard it.

:example capy render
ob_start()
print("buffered")
print(ob_get_close(), "\n")

:see
>ob
ob_get_close
ob_close
