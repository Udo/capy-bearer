:sig
DValue::keys() (legacy `StringList` page)

:content
Removed as a separate StringList API. `DValue::keys()` returns a list-shaped DValue of ordered child keys.

:see
0_DValue
2_DValue_keys

:example capy render
var items := split("one,two", ",")
print(join(keys(items), ","), "\n")
:example cpp render
DValue items; DValue one; one = "one"; DValue two; two = "two";
items.push(one); items.push(two);
DValue item_keys = items.keys();
print(item_keys[0].to_string(), ",", item_keys[1].to_string(), "\n");
